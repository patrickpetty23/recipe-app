# Customer Focus and Positioning

## Primary Customer Segment

- iPhone users who cook multiple times weekly
- Mix physical cookbooks with screenshot-based recipes
- Want less planning overhead before grocery trips

## Secondary Segment

- Students and young professionals batch-cooking from social media recipes

## Customer Alternatives Today

1. Notes app manual copy
2. Reminders checklist manual copy
3. Generic OCR then manual cleanup
4. Recipe apps requiring full recipe input

## Positioning Statement

For home cooks who save recipes in messy formats, Recipe Scanner is the fastest path from recipe image to editable shopping checklist, without forcing full manual entry.

## Differentiation

1. Camera/screenshot ingestion as first-class input
2. Ingredient-specific parsing instead of generic OCR dump
3. Immediate editability and persistence
4. Offline-first default behavior
5. AI meal identification from food photos

## Why This Can Succeed

1. Focused job-to-be-done with short time-to-value
2. Minimal setup required compared with recipe library apps
3. Practical value appears in first session, not after long onboarding
